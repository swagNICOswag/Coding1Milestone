PlayStation 4 (2013)

Release Date: November 15, 2013 (North America); November 29, 2013 (Europe); February 22, 2014 (Japan). Manufacturer: Sony Computer Entertainment. Processor: AMD Jaguar x86-64, 8 cores. Graphics: AMD GCN-based Radeon, 1.84 teraflops. RAM: 8 GB GDDR5. Storage Options: 500 GB and 1 TB hard drives. Optical Drive: Blu-ray/DVD drive (supports 4K video streaming, but not 4K UHD Blu-ray discs). Resolution Support: 1080p (Full HD) resolution natively. 4K video output for media streaming and gaming (via updates in later models). Video Output: HDMI 1.4, supports up to 4K video output at 30Hz. DualShock 4 Controller: Wireless connectivity (Bluetooth). Touchpad, light bar, built-in speaker, and motion-sensing. 3.5mm headphone jack for audio. Online Service: PlayStation Network (PSN), including PS Plus for multiplayer, free monthly games, and cloud storage. Backward Compatibility: No native backward compatibility for PS3 discs, but access to PS3 games through PlayStation Now. PlayStation Camera (Optional): Allows facial recognition, voice commands, and motion control. VR Support: Compatible with PlayStation VR headset. Media Features: Streaming apps like Netflix, YouTube, Hulu, and Spotify. Supports MP3 music playback, video streaming, and USB file storage.
