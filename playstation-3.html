PlayStation 3 (2006)

Release Date: November 11, 2006 (Japan), November 17, 2006 (North America), March 23, 2007 (Europe). Manufacturer: Sony Computer Entertainment. Console Type: Home video game console. Media: Uses Blu-ray Discs for games, which also allows for high-definition movie playback. CPU: Cell Broadband Engine, developed by IBM, Sony, and Toshiba. A powerful processor with a unique architecture that enabled advanced gaming capabilities. Graphics: NVIDIA RSX "Reality Synthesizer" GPU, capable of rendering high-definition graphics up to 1080p. Sound: Supports advanced audio technologies, including Dolby TrueHD and DTS-HD Master Audio for immersive sound. Backward Compatibility: Initially offered backward compatibility with some PlayStation 2 games (through hardware or software emulation) in early models, later models removed this feature. Online Services: Introduced the PlayStation Network (PSN) for digital downloads, multiplayer gaming, and access to digital media services like movies and TV shows. Controller: DualShock 3 controller, which added wireless functionality and rumble feedback, along with the Sixaxis motion-sensing capabilities. Storage: Offered models with hard drives ranging from 20GB up to 500GB, providing ample space for game saves, downloadable content, and multimedia. Game Library: Over 1,000 games released, including iconic titles like The Last of Us, Uncharted: Drake's Fortune, Gran Turismo 5, LittleBigPlanet, Metal Gear Solid 4, and God of War III.
