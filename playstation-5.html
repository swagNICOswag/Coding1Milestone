PlayStation 5 (2020)

Release Date: November 12, 2020 (North America, Japan, Australia, and South Korea); November 19, 2020 (Worldwide). CPU: Custom AMD Zen 2 architecture, 8 cores at 3.5 GHz. GPU: Custom RDNA 2 architecture from AMD, capable of 4K gaming at 120Hz and ray tracing support. RAM: 16 GB GDDR6. Storage: 825 GB SSD (expandable with NVMe SSD slot). Bandwidth: 5.5 GB/s raw (9 GB/s compressed). Performance: 4K Resolution: Native 4K at up to 120 Hz. Ray Tracing: Hardware support for ray tracing, providing more realistic lighting, shadows, and reflections. Frame Rates: Up to 120 frames per second (FPS) in supported games. Quick Resume: Game loading times are significantly reduced due to the fast SSD. Backward Compatibility: Supports most PS4 games (both physical and digital). Enhanced performance on select PS4 titles, with faster load times and better frame rates. DualSense Controller: Adaptive Triggers: R2 and L2 buttons can change resistance depending on in-game actions (e.g., pulling a bowstring or firing a weapon). Built-in Microphone: Allows for voice communication without a headset. USB-C charging and improved battery life.
