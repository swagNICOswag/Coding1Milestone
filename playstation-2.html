PlayStation 2 (2000)

Release Date: March 4, 2000 (Japan), October 26, 2000 (North America), November 30, 2000 (Europe). Manufacturer: Sony Computer Entertainment. Media: Uses DVD-ROM discs for games, and can also play DVDs, making it one of the first consoles with DVD playback capabilities. CPU: Emotion Engine (128-bit) custom-designed by Sony. Graphics: Graphics Synthesizer (GS) with support for advanced rendering, including real-time 3D graphics and impressive visual effects. Sound: Supports multi-channel audio through a dedicated sound processor. Backward Compatibility: Initially compatible with PlayStation 1 games, making it easy for players to transition to the new console. Game Library: Over 1,500 games released, including iconic titles like Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas, Final Fantasy X, Gran Turismo 3, Metal Gear Solid 2, and Tekken 5. Best-Selling Console: The PlayStation 2 is the best-selling game console of all time, with over 155 million units sold globally. Online Play: Introduced online multiplayer capabilities in some games through an Ethernet adapter or built-in network ports. Graphics and Performance: Known for impressive graphics and performance for its time, capable of 480p resolution with component video output in some games. Controller: The DualShock 2 controller, featuring analog buttons, rumble feedback, and ergonomic design. Models and Variants: Several versions were released, including a slim model in 2004, which was smaller and lighter than the original model.
