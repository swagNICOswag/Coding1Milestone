Original PlayStation (1994)

Release Date: Japan: December 3, 1994. North America: September 9, 1995. Europe: September 29, 1995. Design: Compact, gray rectangular console. Two controller ports. No internal hard drive. CD-ROM drive for game media. Graphics: GPU capable of 360,000 polygons per second (varied by game). Supported 2D and 3D graphics rendering. Video output: Composite video, S-Video, and RGB. Memory: 2 MB of RAM for general use. 1 MB of VRAM (Video RAM) for graphics processing. Storage: No internal storage; games were saved on memory cards (1 MB). Memory cards inserted into the front of the console. Controller: Wired controller with a directional pad (D-pad), four face buttons (Circle, Cross, Square, Triangle), two shoulder buttons, and a start/select button. Early models did not have analog sticks. Later, DualShock controller with analog sticks and rumble feedback was introduced in 1997. Games: Used proprietary 700 MB CD-ROM format for games. Over 7,900 game titles released during its lifespan. Iconic games: Gran Turismo, Final Fantasy VII, Metal Gear Solid, Crash Bandicoot, Tekken 3.
